DESCRIPTION
A hiker took a photo of a mountain. Years later, four pixels in that photo got him killed.

This is the complete story of SCP-096, often called "The Shy Guy": what it is, the one rule you can never break, how the Foundation contains it, the disastrous capture mission, the breach that ended on a highway, the questions nobody can answer, and why it has never been destroyed.
